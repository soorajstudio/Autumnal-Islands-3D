野点 • AUTUMN TEA RITUAL

The Art of Solitary Tea in Autumn

In Japanese aesthetics, nodate (野点) is the practice of conducting a tea ceremony out in nature. Here on the wooden bench, facing the falling maple leaves, every element modeled in 3D reflects the spirit of ichi-go ichi-e ("one time, one meeting").

  • 🍵
    Matcha in Stoneware Chawan
    First-harvest shade-grown green tea leaves stone-ground into vibrant jade powder and whisked with mountain spring water.
  • 🌰
    Autumn Chestnut Wagashi
    A delicate layered confection made of sweet chestnut puree (kuri kinton) and adzuki red bean, balancing the slight astringency of the matcha.
  • 🪵
    Hinoki & Forged Iron Bench
    Warm honey-toned Japanese cypress wood planks joined with dark forged iron rivets, positioned for prime foliage contemplation.

植生と地質 • BOTANICAL & GEOLOGY

Ecology of the Floating Realm

The flora and stone formations represented in the 3D model are drawn from the alpine ecosystems of the Japanese Alps and mythical celestial lore.

Acer palmatum var. matsudae

Japanese Mountain Maple

Characterized by its deep scarlet and orange palmate leaves. In the 3D scene, the foliage is clustered into stylized dodecahedron groupings that catch sunlight from multiple angles.

Cryptomeria japonica

Ancient Sugi Cedar

Dense, fragrant reddish-brown timber forming the structural trunks and bridge beams. Cedar wood naturally resists mountain moisture and rot.

Racomitrium japonicum

Sun Moss & Velvet Lichen

Hardy alpine moss that carpets the stone steps, bridge approaches, and the base of the sacred monument, turning golden olive in autumn.

Basalt & Gravitational Ore

Celestial Sky Basalt

Dark stratified volcanic bedrock containing natural magnetic anomalies that react with atmospheric slipstreams, allowing the satellite islands to levitate indefinitely.
